# InniSync design direction

Written from the project owner's answers on 2026-10-31. This file is the direction for every screen; change it here first.

## Identity
- **Name:** InniSync, a desktop companion for the Innioasis Y1 (unofficial, not affiliated with Innioasis).
- **Same look on every computer** (macOS, Windows, Linux). It does not imitate any one operating system.

## Feel
- **Calm and quiet**, a well-made tool that stays out of the way.
- Dial: ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1.

## Theme
- **Light and dark**, following the computer's light/dark setting. Both must be complete.

## Colour
- **One accent colour: the user's Y1 colour**, chosen in Preferences (the nine official Y1 colours).
- **Plain grey** is offered as an accent choice for people who don't want it to match their Y1.
- The accent is used sparingly: selection, primary buttons, progress.

## Type
- **Atkinson Hyperlegible Next**, font files bundled with the app (nothing loaded from the web).

## Icon
- **A simple click wheel**, drawn fresh, with a plain centre button (no Innioasis logo), tinted in the accent colour.

## Layout
- Sidebar on the left: Library, the Y1, Playlists, Mixes.
- Song list on the right.
- A slim now-playing bar.
- The Y1's pages (Summary, Music, Podcasts, and the rest) open in the main area.
